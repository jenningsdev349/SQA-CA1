# Document Goals and Purpose

This project handbook is a hub of reading material for team members to read and learn from. The goals of this handbook are to:
- Cover each topic with an easy-to-read, accessible approach
- Inform the reader of each topic, so they can be a valuable team contributor
- Use a variety of sources and further reading materials (articles, blog posts) to increase the reader's engagement with articles 
- Cover good practices, discuss their value, and sufficiently cover the pitfalls of bad practices 

# Project Structure

The handbook material is split between three articles covering different topics. The topics covered by each article are: 
- #### Task Estimation
- #### Code Reviews
- #### Code Styling

Each article follows a format of:
- #### Introduction
- #### Article Content
- #### Conclusion
- #### Sources
