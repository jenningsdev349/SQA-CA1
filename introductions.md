# Document Goals and Purpose

This project handbook is a hub of reading material for team members to read and learn from. The goals of this handbook are to:
- Cover each topic with an easy-to-read, accessible approach
- Inform the reader of each topic to a standard to which they would be a valuable team contributor
- Use a variety of content (articles, blog posts) to use as sources, providing more personalised and anecdotal articles
- Cover good practices, discuss their value, and sufficiently cover the pitfalls of bad practices 

# Project Structure

The handbook material is split between 3 articles covering different respective topics. The topics covered by each article are: 
- #### Task Estimation
- #### Code Reviews
- #### Code Styling

Each article follows an format of:
- #### Introduction
- #### Article Content
- #### Conclusion
- #### Sources

Articles are written in markdown format and are placed in the /articles/ folder. Images used within articles exist in the /images/ folder.
